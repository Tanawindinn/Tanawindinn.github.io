November 20, 2025

Vitebsk Logistics Forum

On November 20, 2025, the company's management participated in the Vitebsk Logistics Forum "Product + Destination = Export"! The event was attended by managers and specialists from industrial enterprises and logistics companies, as well as representatives from the legal, financial, insurance, and IT sectors of Belarus. The Forum discussed practical solutions for logistics and freight transportation, participants exchanged experiences and gained new business contacts. BELATEKAVTO Director Alexander Bondarenko was a speaker at one of the sessions.
